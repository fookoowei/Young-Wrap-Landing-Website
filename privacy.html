Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This page explains what happens to your data when you use this website. The short version: we keep it minimal — no accounts, no tracking cookies, no ads, and nothing you type here is stored on our servers.

Who we are

Young Wrap is a car wrap and detailing shop at 42, Jalan Anggerik Vanilla Ad 31/Ad, Kota Kemuning, 40460 Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia. This website is our online showcase and the easiest way to reach us.

What we collect

This website has no accounts and no database. When you fill in the quote form or tap a WhatsApp button, your message is composed on your own device and opens in WhatsApp — nothing is sent or saved anywhere until you press send inside WhatsApp. We then receive it as a normal WhatsApp chat, handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies & local storage

This site sets no tracking cookies of its own. Your browser's local storage is used only to remember your language choice (English or 中文), that you've already seen the intro animation this session, and that you've dismissed the cookie notice. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party content

Some parts of this site load content from third parties: Instagram posts from creators we've worked with, a Google Maps view of our shop, and web fonts from Google Fonts. When those load, your browser contacts those providers, which may log technical data (such as your IP address) and set their own cookies under their policies:

  • Instagram / Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy (Maps & Fonts)
  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy

Analytics & advertising

We run no analytics, no ad trackers and no marketing pixels on this website.

Your choices

You can block third-party cookies in your browser without breaking this site. If you'd like anything you've sent us over WhatsApp corrected or deleted, just ask in the same chat and we'll take care of it.
